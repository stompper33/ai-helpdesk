# Troubleshooting: Bluetooth connections

General steps for any Bluetooth device we sell (headphones, smart watch). Product guides have model-specific steps.

## Device won't pair
Turn Bluetooth off and on again on the phone or computer, put the device in pairing mode (usually holding the power button until a light flashes), and keep it within 1 metre. If the device was paired before, remove it from the Bluetooth list ("forget this device") and pair again.

## Keeps disconnecting
Bluetooth range is about 10 metres without walls. Microwaves, Wi-Fi routers and USB 3 hubs can interfere. Keep the device firmware and the computer's Bluetooth drivers up to date, and turn off battery saving modes that switch Bluetooth off.

## Connected but no sound
Check that the device is chosen as the audio output on the phone or computer, and that the volume is up on both. On Windows, choose the device as the default playback device in the sound settings.

## Pairing with several devices
Many devices remember several phones and computers, but can play audio from only one at a time. Disconnect it from the other device first.
