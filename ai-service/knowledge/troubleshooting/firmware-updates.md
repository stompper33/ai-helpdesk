# Troubleshooting: firmware updates

How to update device firmware safely and what to do when an update fails. Applies to headphones, docking stations, watches and laptops.

## Updating safely
Charge the device to at least 50% (or keep it plugged in), keep it close to the phone or computer running the update, and do not close the app or turn the device off until the update says it is complete. Updates usually take 5-15 minutes.

## Update failed or device won't start
Interrupted updates are the most common cause of a device that won't start or only shows a logo. Most devices have a recovery mode that reinstalls the firmware; see the product guide for the exact steps. If recovery does not work, the device is covered as a defect: under the returns policy within 30 days of delivery, and under the warranty after that. A failed update is never treated as accidental damage.

## Why updates matter
Firmware updates fix known problems such as crackling audio on headphones or random disconnects on docking stations, so checking for an update is one of the first troubleshooting steps.
