# Account security policy

How support handles password problems, suspicious activity and identity checks.

## Password reset email not arriving
Password reset emails come from no-reply@helpdesk.example. Ask the customer to check their spam or junk folder and to add that address to their contacts. Some work email systems block it; support can trigger a reset from a backup sender if it still does not arrive after 15 minutes.

## Compromised or hacked account
If an account may be compromised (orders the customer did not place, a changed email address, unknown logins), treat it as the highest priority: support locks the account, cancels any unshipped orders the customer did not place, and asks the customer to reset their password and turn on two-factor authentication. Charges for fraudulent orders are refunded once confirmed.

## Verifying identity
Before changing account details, confirm the customer's identity by replying to the email address on the account. Never confirm or change account details for a request from an unverified email address.

## What never to ask for
Never ask for a customer's password, a one-time security code, or a full card number, and never send these in an email.
