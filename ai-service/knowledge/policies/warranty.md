# Warranty policy

What the manufacturer warranty covers and how warranty claims work.

## Coverage
All products have a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date (laptops: 2 years). It covers defects in materials and workmanship, such as a component that stops working in normal use. It does not cover accidental damage (drops, liquid spills, cracked screens), normal wear such as worn ear cushions, or damage from unofficial repairs.

## Faults in the first 30 days
A defect within the first 30 days after delivery is handled as a return or replacement under the returns policy, which is faster than a warranty repair.

## Faults after 30 days
After 30 days, we arrange a warranty repair or replacement. The customer may need to provide a short description or a video of the fault and the order number. Repairs take about 7-10 business days, including shipping both ways, and shipping is free.

## Proof of purchase
The order number is enough for anything bought from our store. Items bought elsewhere need a receipt and are handled by the manufacturer directly.
