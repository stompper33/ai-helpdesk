# Shipping and delivery policy

Delivery times, tracking problems and lost parcels for domestic orders. International orders: see the international shipping FAQ.

## Delivery times
Orders ship within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping then takes 3-5 business days; express shipping takes 1-2 business days. We cannot guarantee delivery by a specific date, because carriers set the final timing.

## Tracking has not updated
Tracking often pauses for a day or two while a parcel moves between depots. If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, we open a trace with the carrier, which takes up to 3 business days. If the parcel is not found, we reship the order or refund it, whichever the customer prefers.

## Delivered but not received
If tracking says delivered but the customer has nothing, ask them to check with neighbours, around the property, and with their building's mail room or reception, and to wait 48 hours, because carriers sometimes mark parcels delivered early. After 48 hours we open a carrier claim and reship or refund once the claim is confirmed.

## Urgent deliveries and express upgrades
When a customer needs an item by a deadline, we can upgrade a reshipment or replacement to express shipping at no cost. We cannot speed up a parcel that is already with the carrier.

## Changing the delivery address
The address can be changed only before the order ships. After that, the customer can ask the carrier to redirect the parcel using the tracking link.
