# Support hours and contact

When and how customers can reach the support team.

## Support hours
Support is available Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm Eastern time, excluding public holidays. Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next business day.

## Response times
We aim to reply to every ticket within one business day. Urgent issues (hacked accounts, safety concerns) are handled first.

## Contact options
Customers can open a ticket from their account page or email support. There is no phone support; a support agent can arrange a call back for complex cases.

## Feedback and complaints
Compliments and complaints are passed to the team lead. A customer who is unhappy with how a ticket was handled can ask for it to be escalated to a team lead.
