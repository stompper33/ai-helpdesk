# Returns and replacements policy

How returns, exchanges and replacements work at the store. All timelines count from the delivery date.

## Return window for unwanted items
Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for any reason (change of mind, wrong size, no longer needed). The customer pays return shipping for change-of-mind returns, and the refund is issued once the warehouse has inspected the item. Opened software, gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned.

## Defective, damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives broken, damaged in transit, faulty, or different from what was ordered (wrong model, size or colour), it can be returned or replaced within 30 days of delivery at no cost to the customer. We email a prepaid return label. Ask the customer for a short description and, if possible, a photo of the damage or the wrong item, plus the order number.

## Replacements
A replacement ships once the warehouse confirms stock, usually within 2 business days, as a new order linked to the original (order numbers ending in -R1, -R2). The customer can keep using the faulty item until the replacement arrives and then return it with the prepaid label. If the item is out of stock, offer a refund instead.

## Returns after 30 days
After 30 days, unwanted items can no longer be returned. Faulty items are handled under the warranty policy instead.

## Approval
Returns, replacements and refunds must be approved by a support agent before they are promised to the customer. In a reply, say the team will review the request.
