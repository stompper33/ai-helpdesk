# Refunds policy

When and how money is returned to customers.

## Refund timelines
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. Banks can take a few extra days to show the credit on a statement. Refunds are never paid in cash, by bank transfer to a different account, or as gift cards unless the customer asks for store credit.

## Charged for a cancelled order
If an order was cancelled before it shipped, the charge the customer sees is usually a pending authorization (a temporary hold), not a real payment. It drops off automatically within 3-5 business days. If the charge was actually captured (it shows as completed, not pending), we refund it within 5 business days of confirming the cancellation.

## Double charges
If a customer was charged twice for one order, ask for the dates and amounts of both charges and the last 4 digits of the card. Duplicate captured charges are refunded in full once confirmed.

## Partial refunds
Partial refunds are possible when only part of an order is returned, or as a goodwill gesture for a late delivery. Shipping costs are refunded only when the return is our fault (defective, damaged or wrong item).

## Card details
Never ask the customer for their full card number, CVV or online banking details. The last 4 digits of the card are enough to find a payment.

## Approval
Refunds must be approved by a support agent before they are promised to the customer.
