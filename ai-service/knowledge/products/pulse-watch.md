# Pulse S2 smart watch

A smart watch with heart-rate tracking, GPS and notifications, paired with the Pulse app on iPhone or Android. Sold as "Smart watch".

## Firmware update failed or watch stuck on logo
If an update was interrupted and the watch only shows the logo, put it on the charger, then hold the side button for 15 seconds until it vibrates. This starts recovery mode, which reinstalls the firmware through the Pulse app; keep the phone close and the app open. If recovery mode does not start, the watch is covered as a defect: under the returns policy within 30 days of delivery, under the warranty after that.

## Pairing with a phone
Install the Pulse app, turn on Bluetooth and location on the phone, then follow the steps in the app. If pairing fails, remove "Pulse S2" from the phone's Bluetooth list, restart the watch and try again. The watch can be paired with only one phone at a time.

## Battery drains quickly
Normal battery life is about 5 days, or 12 hours with GPS on continuously. The always-on display and blood-oxygen tracking roughly halve battery life. If battery life drops below 2 days with those features off, the battery may be faulty and is covered by the warranty.

## Notifications not showing
Allow notifications for the Pulse app in the phone's settings, and turn off the phone's battery optimisation for the Pulse app (Android), because it stops notifications from reaching the watch.
