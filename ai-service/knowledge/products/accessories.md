# Accessories: phone cases and laptop sleeves

Phone cases and the laptop sleeve sold alongside our devices.

## Phone case fit
Each case fits only the phone model named on the box. If a case does not fit, check the phone model in the phone's settings; a case for the wrong model can be exchanged within 30 days if unused and in its packaging.

## Laptop sleeve sizes
The laptop sleeve fits laptops up to 14 inches, including the Stratus 14. For a 15- or 16-inch laptop, the large sleeve is needed. The sleeve is padded but not waterproof.

## Cleaning and care
Wipe cases and sleeves with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not use alcohol or bleach, which yellow clear cases. Yellowing of clear cases over time is normal wear and not a defect.

## Accessory warranty
Accessories have the standard 1-year warranty against defects such as broken stitching or a cracked case edge in normal use, but not against damage from drops.
