# Aurora ANC-700 noise-cancelling headphones

Wireless over-ear Bluetooth headphones with active noise cancelling (ANC), sold as "Noise-cancelling headphones". Up to 30 hours of battery life with ANC on.

## Pairing with a phone or laptop
Turn the headphones off, then hold the power button for 5 seconds until the light flashes blue. Select "Aurora ANC-700" in the Bluetooth settings of the phone or laptop. On Windows laptops, use Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Add device. The headphones remember up to 8 devices.

## Crackling, popping or distorted sound
Crackling in one or both earcups, especially at high volume, is usually fixed by firmware version 2.1 or later, installed with the Aurora Sound app. After updating, reset the headphones by holding the power and ANC buttons together for 10 seconds. If crackling continues after the update and reset, it is a hardware fault: handle it as a return within 30 days of delivery, or as a warranty claim after that.

## Noise cancelling not working well
Make sure ANC mode is on (press the ANC button; a voice prompt says "noise cancelling on"). The ear cushions must seal around the ears, so glasses or worn cushions reduce the effect. Replacement ear cushions are sold separately; worn cushions are normal wear and not covered by the warranty.

## Battery and charging
A full charge takes about 2 hours over USB-C. If the headphones do not charge, try a different cable and power adapter. Battery life shorter than 20 hours with ANC on, within the first year, is covered by the warranty.

## Only one side plays sound
Check the balance setting on the phone or laptop, then reset the headphones as described above. If one side still has no sound, it is a hardware fault.
