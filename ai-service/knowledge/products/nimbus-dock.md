# Nimbus D4 USB-C docking station

A USB-C docking station with two HDMI ports, one DisplayPort, Ethernet, four USB ports and up to 96 W of laptop charging, sold as "USB-C docking station".

## External monitors not detected
Connect the dock with the USB-C cable that came in the box, plugged into a laptop port that supports video (marked with a DisplayPort or Thunderbolt symbol). Not every USB-C port supports video. On Windows, install the Nimbus DisplayLink driver from the product page to use two or three monitors. MacBooks with M1 or M2 base chips support only one external display without the driver.

## Laptop not charging through the dock
The dock delivers up to 96 W, but only when powered by its own 120 W adapter. Laptops that need more than 96 W charge slowly or show a "slow charger" warning; that is expected. Check that the adapter's light is on and that the USB-C cable is the original one.

## Dock disconnects or flickers
Update the dock firmware with the Nimbus Updater tool (version 1.8 or later fixes most random disconnects), update the laptop's graphics and USB drivers, and avoid USB-C extension cables.

## Ethernet and USB devices not working
Unplug the dock's power for 30 seconds to reset it. If the Ethernet port shows no lights, try another network cable. Devices that need a lot of power, such as external hard drives, should use the blue USB ports.
