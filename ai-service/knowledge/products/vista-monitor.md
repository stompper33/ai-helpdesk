# Vista 27" 4K monitor (V27-UHD)

A 27-inch 3840x2160 IPS monitor with HDMI 2.0, DisplayPort 1.4 and USB-C with 65 W charging, sold as "27-inch 4K monitor". Smaller 24-inch models are sold separately.

## No signal
Check that the cable is firmly connected at both ends, then press the Input button on the back of the monitor to select the right source (HDMI, DisplayPort or USB-C). For USB-C, the laptop port must support video. Try another cable before assuming a fault.

## Not running at 4K or 60 Hz
4K at 60 Hz needs DisplayPort, a USB-C port with video, or an HDMI cable labelled "Premium High Speed". Older HDMI cables limit the picture to 30 Hz, which makes the mouse feel slow. In the computer's display settings, choose 3840x2160 at 60 Hz.

## Dead or stuck pixels
A few pixels that stay black or one colour can occur on any LCD. Under our pixel policy, a monitor with 3 or more dead or stuck pixels, or any dead pixel in the centre of the screen, is replaced within the warranty period.

## Received the wrong size
If a 24-inch monitor arrived instead of the 27-inch model ordered (or the other way round), it is a wrong item: it is replaced under the returns policy within 30 days of delivery, with a prepaid return label.
