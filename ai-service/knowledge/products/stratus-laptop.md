# Stratus 14 laptop

A 14-inch laptop with 16 GB of memory, a 1 TB SSD and USB-C charging, sold as "14-inch laptop". It has a 2-year warranty.

## Battery care and battery life
Expect 10-12 hours of light use. Battery Care mode (in the Stratus Control app) stops charging at 80% to extend battery lifespan; turn it off before travelling for a full charge. Battery capacity below 80% within the 2-year warranty is covered.

## Laptop will not turn on
Connect the original charger for 30 minutes, then hold the power button for 20 seconds to force a restart. If the charging light does not come on, try another USB-C power outlet. If it still does not start, it is handled under the returns policy within 30 days of delivery, or the warranty after that.

## BIOS and driver updates
Install updates through the Stratus Control app. Keep the laptop plugged in during a BIOS update and do not close the lid; an interrupted BIOS update can stop the laptop from starting and needs a warranty repair.

## Registering the warranty
The warranty starts automatically from the delivery date of orders from our store; registration is not needed. The serial number is on the bottom of the laptop and in the Stratus Control app.
