# Support policies

Fictional policies for the demo store. The agent may only quote facts from these
sections; it must never invent its own. Each "## topic" heading is one lookup key.

## returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
Items that arrive defective, damaged, or different from what was ordered can be returned
or replaced within 30 days of delivery at no cost; we email a prepaid return label.
A replacement ships once the warehouse confirms stock, usually within 2 business days.
Replacements and returns must be approved by a support agent before they are promised.

## refunds
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
If an order was cancelled before shipping, the charge is usually a pending authorization
that drops off within 3-5 business days. If the charge was actually captured, we refund it
within 5 business days of confirming the cancellation.
Refunds must be approved by a support agent before they are promised to the customer.
Never ask the customer for their full card number; the last 4 digits are enough.

## shipping
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days after an order ships.
If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, we open a trace with the carrier, which
takes up to 3 business days. If the parcel is not found, we reship or refund.
If tracking says delivered but the customer has nothing, ask them to check with neighbours
and their building's mail room and wait 48 hours, then we open a carrier claim.
We cannot guarantee delivery by a specific date, but can upgrade to express on a reshipment.

## warranty
All products have a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date covering defects,
not accidental damage. Within the first 30 days, defects are handled as a return (see returns).
After 30 days, we arrange a warranty repair or replacement; the customer may need to provide
a short description or video of the fault.

## account_security
Password reset emails come from no-reply@helpdesk.example; ask the customer to check spam and
to allow that address. Support can trigger a reset from a backup sender.
If an account may be compromised, support locks the account, cancels unshipped orders the
customer did not place, and asks them to reset their password. Treat as the highest priority.
Never ask for a customer's password, and never confirm account details to an unverified email.

## technical_support
Most pairing and connection problems are fixed by resetting the device and re-pairing.
Firmware updates must not be interrupted; if an update fails and the device won't start,
it is covered as a defect (see warranty or returns, depending on how long since delivery).
Setup guides are available on the product page.

## general
Support hours are Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm Eastern. We aim to reply within one
business day. Gift cards are sold on the website in amounts from $25 to $200.
