# FAQ: gift cards and promotions

Buying and using gift cards, discount codes and promotions.

## Buying gift cards
Digital gift cards are sold on the website in amounts from $25 to $200 and are emailed to the recipient on a chosen date. Gift cards do not expire and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Using a gift card
Enter the gift card code at checkout. Several gift cards can be combined in one order, and any remaining balance stays on the card for later.

## Lost gift card code
If the recipient lost the email, the buyer can resend it from their order page. Support can also resend it to the original recipient address with the order number.

## Discount codes
Only one discount code can be used per order, and codes cannot be applied after an order is placed. Discount codes cannot be used to buy gift cards.
