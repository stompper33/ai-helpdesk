# FAQ: orders and payment

Common questions about placing, changing and paying for orders.

## Cancelling an order
An order can be cancelled from the account page until it ships, which is usually within 1-2 business days. Once it has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned within 30 days of delivery.

## Changing an order
Items, quantities and the delivery address can be changed only before the order ships. Support can make the change if the customer contacts us quickly; otherwise cancel and reorder.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and store gift cards. Payment is taken when the order ships; until then the customer sees a pending authorization.

## Invoices and VAT invoices for companies
An invoice is emailed when the order ships and can be downloaded from the account page. Business customers who need a VAT invoice with a company name and VAT number can request one from support with the order number; it is issued within 2 business days. Company details cannot be added after the invoice's month has closed.

## Price drops after purchase
If the price drops within 14 days of the order, the customer can ask for the difference as store credit.
