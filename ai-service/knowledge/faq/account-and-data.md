# FAQ: account and personal data

Managing an account, login settings and personal data.

## Changing the email address
The email address can be changed on the account page under Login & security. A confirmation link is sent to the new address and a notice to the old one. If the customer can no longer access the old address, support must verify their identity first (see the account security policy).

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a one-time code from an authenticator app at login. Turn it on under Login & security. A customer who lost their phone can use one of the backup codes shown when 2FA was set up; otherwise support resets 2FA after verifying identity.

## Deleting an account
Customers can ask support to delete their account and personal data. Order records are kept for 7 years for tax purposes, without marketing data. Deletion is completed within 30 days and cannot be undone.

## Marketing emails
Customers can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link at the bottom of any email or in the account settings. Order and shipping emails cannot be turned off.
