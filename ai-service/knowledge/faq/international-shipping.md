# FAQ: international shipping and customs

Shipping to customers outside the United States.

## Countries we ship to
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. International orders take 7-14 business days after shipping, depending on customs.

## Customs duties and import taxes
For the UK and EU, prices at checkout include import VAT and duties, so nothing extra is due on delivery. For Canada, the carrier may collect duties and taxes on delivery; these are the customer's responsibility.

## Parcel held at customs
If tracking shows the parcel is held at customs, the carrier usually contacts the customer for details or payment. Ask the customer to check for an email or text from the carrier. Parcels held for more than 10 business days are traced by support.

## International returns
International customers can return items within 30 days of delivery. For defective, damaged or wrong items, we email a prepaid international return label; for change-of-mind returns, the customer arranges and pays for shipping.
